## 4. Open Questions / Assumptions Made

- **Assumption 3 (Root landing route):** The landing page `/` is handled within `app/routes/auth.py` so that no extraneous blueprints or route modules were introduced beyond the exact 6 specified in `architecture.md`.
- **Assumption 4 (Password length):** Minimum password length is enforced at 6 characters on both client and server validation layers.
- **Assumption 5 (Child permissions):** Only parents (and platform admins) can edit or delete child profiles. Assigned educators have view-only access to learner profiles.
- **Assumption 6 (Admin self-protection):** Admins are prevented from deactivating their own account or changing their own role away from admin to avoid platform lockout.
- **Assumption 7 (Teacher assignments):** An admin interface at `/admin/assignments` handles pairing teachers and students with a unique constraint on `(teacher_id, child_id)` preventing duplicates.
- **Assumption 8 (Temporary scoring in Phase 3):** Scoring was handled temporarily via Flask session state during Phase 3 play-through before persistent session/event logs were added in Phase 4.
- **Assumption 9 (Question options storage):** Question choices are stored as JSON arrays in `options_json` with a convenient `.options` Python model property.
- **Assumption 10 (Child results tone):** The activity results screen displays encouragement stars and positive praise without raw percentage scores or clinical evaluations.
- **Assumption 11 (Session superseding on restart):** If a child starts an activity with an older session left `in_progress`, the old session is automatically closed and marked `abandoned` with elapsed duration before starting the new session.
- **Assumption 12 (Question skip scoring):** Skipping a question counts as an attempt without adding a correct answer, and logs a `skipped` event for learning assistance analytics.
- **Assumption 13 (Consistency score variance penalty):** Consistency is computed from the standard deviation of accuracy across sessions, with penalty `min(50.0, std_dev) * 2.0`, giving 100.0 for stable performance and scaling down to 0.0 for wildly erratic scores.
- **Assumption 14 (Engagement index weights):** Engagement index (0-100) sums 7 signals: completion (0.20), inverted abandonment (0.15), repeat participation (0.15), interaction frequency (0.15), time spent (0.15), category breadth (0.10), and persistence/retries (0.10).
- **Assumption 15 (On-demand progress records):** `update_child_progress_records(child_id)` generates and saves `ProgressRecord` entries per category on-demand or via the `/api/progress/<child_id>` endpoint.
- **Assumption 16 (Top 3–5 Recommendations):** The personalized recommendation layer picks the top 3 to 5 activities per child to maintain focused engagement without overwhelming children or parents.
- **Assumption 17 (Sequential Priority Assignment):** Recommendations are assigned ordinal priorities (1..N) where 1 is the most urgent/suitable activity, ensuring deterministic presentation order in parent/child UIs.
- **Assumption 18 (Cold-Start Strategy):** New learners with 0 recorded activity sessions receive introductory Beginner exploration activities across diverse domains to establish baseline performance metrics.
- **Assumption 19 (Stamina vs Challenge Progression):** In the performance-based layer, if a child demonstrates high accuracy (>=80%) but low completion (<50% due to early abandonment), difficulty is held at current level (`reinforce`) to encourage task stamina before leveling up.
- **Assumption 20 (Ethical Term Ban List):** Recommendation reasons are tested against a hard-coded ban list of 16+ clinical/diagnostic terms (`adhd`, `autism`, `dyslexia`, `disorder`, `syndrome`, `deficit`, `abnormal`, `pathology`, `iq`, etc.) to strictly uphold PRD §4.
- **Assumption 21 (Feature Ordering & Dimension Mapping):** Feature vectors follow a fixed 6-dimensional array format: `[visual, memory, logic, number, language, engagement]`, mapping the database category slugs directly to educational domains.
- **Assumption 22 (Minimum Clustering Cohort):** A minimum threshold of 3 children with activity sessions (`MIN_CHILDREN_FOR_CLUSTERING = 3`) is required before fitting K-Means models, falling back to an 'Introductory Exploration' / 'Baseline Interaction Pattern' with graceful explanation when under-threshold.
- **Assumption 23 (Centroid-Driven Dynamic Labelling):** Cluster pattern-group labels are generated programmatically by inspecting the centroid's score spread: spreads <= 12.0 yield balanced interaction, score differences >= 15.0 yield focused single-domain interaction, and dual-leading categories yield combined labels (`Domain1 + Domain2 Strong Interaction`).
