🛡️ Responsible-Use & Ethical Educational Pledge

Educational Guidance Only: ChildInsight is designed strictly as an educational observation and activity recommendation system. It does not diagnose, label, or classify children medically or psychologically, and does not assign personality types or IQ scores.

All metrics reflect activity interaction patterns (accuracy, pace, consistency, engagement) to offer transparent, explainable guidance to parents and educators.

How ChildInsight Works

From playful interaction to actionable educational insights in four transparent steps.

Step 1
🧩

Interactive Play

Children explore structured activities across Visual, Logic, Numbers, Language, and Memory domains at their own pace.

Step 2
⏱️

Event Capture

The platform records interaction flow — choices, response accuracy, duration, hints, and retries — without pressure.

Step 3
📈

Pattern Analytics

Analytics engines convert raw interactions into consistency, engagement, and category performance profiles.

Step 4
💡

Explainable Guidance

Parents and teachers receive tailored recommendations that always explain why a specific activity was suggested.
